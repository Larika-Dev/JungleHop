# Education (Old North State University)

Autoload: `Education="*res://education/education.gd"` (one line in `godot/project.godot`).

## What the player does

1. **Enroll** at the registrar in Founders' Hall (`LM05_FOUNDERS_HALL`) or on the ONSU portal on any computer. Tuition is $450 a course ($800 graduate), paid from `Life.money`. Student loans (up to $40,000, about 5% a year, payments deferred while enrolled) cover a shortfall. At most 5 courses at once. Dropping before any homework is a full refund.
2. **Do homework**, the education mini-game. Open it at any campus building, from a furniture `do_homework` / `write_essay` / `take_online_course` action (desks, computers), or from the registrar. Pick a course, then its next assignment. Each course has **20 homework assignments**, each **10 to 20 multiple choice questions**. Keys 1-4 answer, Enter goes on. Each assignment can be redone once (best score counts, new questions). Quitting mid-assignment records nothing.
3. **Pass** a course with a 60% homework average. Letter grades and a 4.0 GPA follow from that. A failed course can be retaken (pay again).
4. **Graduate** at the registrar: **20 passed courses** with at least 8 in the major (its 4 core courses, 3 at 300-level or above) and GPA 2.0+. A **minor** is 5 to 10 more courses in another subject, counted separately from the 20. A master's (M.S. Computer Science or MBA) is the subject's 5 graduate courses after a qualifying bachelor's. The A.A. General Studies is awarded automatically after 10 lower-level courses in 3 subjects.
5. **Certifications** are single 20-question exams (pass at 70%, fee per attempt) at the Medical Center, Dining Hall, Swain Hall or Carroll Hall.

**Hints** (50/50, max 10) come from studying: library (+1, 60 min), office hours in a course's building (+2, once a day per subject), Cram Fortress Tutoring (+3, $40), study group at the Student Union (+1), and furniture `study` / `attend_class` / `attend_lecture` / `lecture` (+1, 3 a day).

## Catalog: 100 courses across 8 subjects

| Subject | Courses | Building | Minor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mathematics (`math`) | 12 | Pettigrew Hall `ONSU_0000` | 6 |
| Computer Science (`cs`) | 15 (5 graduate) | Swain Hall `ONSU_0011` | 7 |
| Biology (`bio`) | 12 | Hooper Hall `ONSU_0002` | 6 |
| Chemistry (`chem`) | 11 | Venable Hall `ONSU_0020` | 5 |
| Nursing (`nursing`) | 12 | Medical Tower B `LM13_MED_TOWER_B` | 8 |
| Business & Economics (`business`) | 15 (5 graduate) | Carroll Hall `ONSU_0013` | 6 |
| History (`history`) | 12 | Saunders Hall `ONSU_0007` | 5 |
| English (`english`) | 11 | Bynum Hall `ONSU_0008` | 5 |

Courses and rules live in `catalog.gd`. Questions are generated by `questions/gen_<subject>.gd` (about 160 topics: computed problems like equations, dosages, pH, binary, supply and demand, plus fact banks), so a course never runs out of questions. The same course, assignment and attempt always give the same questions, so saves stay small.

## Contract for other systems

```gdscript
Education.has_credential("bs_nursing")      # also accepts "high_school" (always true), "bachelor_any", "masters_any"
Education.has_tag("degree:bachelor")         # degree:bachelor | degree:master | degree:associate | cert | minor | field:<subject>
Education.get_credentials()                  # Array of ids
Education.credential_earned                  # signal(credential_id)
Education.course_completed                   # signal(course_id, passed, percent)
Education.homework_submitted                 # signal(course_id, index, percent)
Education.open_homework() / open_registrar() / open_cert_exams()
```

Credential ids: `bs_mathematics`, `bs_computer_science`, `bs_biology`, `bs_chemistry`, `bs_nursing`, `ba_business`, `ba_history`, `ba_english`, `minor_<subject>`, `ms_computer_science`, `mba`, `aa_general`, `cert_cpr`, `cert_phlebotomy`, `cert_food_safety`, `cert_barista`, `cert_bookkeeping`, `cert_it_support`.

Life flags (prefix `edu.`): `edu.enrolled`, `edu.student`, `edu.major`, `edu.minor`, `edu.gpa`, `edu.courses_passed`, `edu.credentials`, `edu.loan_balance`, and `edu.<credential_id> = true`.

Life actions registered (category `school`): `edu_registrar`, `edu_portal` (computers), `edu_homework`, `edu_study_library`, `edu_office_hours`, `edu_tutoring`, `edu_study_group`, `edu_cert_exam`, `edu_graduate`, `edu_pay_loan`. Location checks use `Life.place.id` or `CityData.room_at(player)`; when the location is unknown they never block.

Saving: registers with `GameState.register_saveable("education", self)` (falls back to `Life.register_save_provider`).
